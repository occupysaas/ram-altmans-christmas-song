# Brief: the vintage console TV plate

Generate ONE image with your image_gen tool and save the processed result as described. Look at it; regenerate if it has any text, logos, brand names or numbers, or if the screen area is not a clean flat magenta. Do not ask questions.

## Style (most important)
A 1960s made-for-TV Christmas cartoon special look: thin, slightly wobbly black ink outlines, flat cel colors with no gradients on characters, watercolor-wash backgrounds with dry-brush texture, curly swooping shapes, exaggerated spindly limbs, slightly limited palette (deep teal-blue night, snow white with lavender shadow, holly red, warm window yellow, sickly villain green). It must be an ORIGINAL design: do not copy the Grinch, Whoville, Scrooge McDuck or any existing character.
No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in any image.


The image: a front-on, centred 1960s wooden console television set filling a 16:9 frame, sitting in a cosy festive living room at night. Warm walnut cabinet with a speaker grille, two chunky dials and tapered legs; a curved glass screen bezel framing a 4:3 screen. Around it: tinsel garland draped over the top, a small plate of cookies and a bowl of nuts on the cabinet, a soft glow of a Christmas tree at the left edge, snowy window at the right edge, warm lamplight. The 4:3 screen area itself must be filled with solid flat magenta #FF00FF (nothing else there); never use magenta anywhere else.

Processing (write `scripts/make_tv.py`, run it with `.venv/bin/python`):
- Resize/crop the result to exactly 1920x1080 (no stretching).
- Make every magenta-ish pixel inside the screen fully transparent (alpha 0); clean any magenta fringe on the bezel edge.
- Find the largest axis-aligned 4:3 rectangle that is fully transparent and centred in the hole; write it as `public/tv/console.json` = {"x":…, "y":…, "w":…, "h":…} (integers, w/h = 4/3 within 1%).
- Make sure pixels 20 px outside that rectangle on every side are fully opaque (alpha 255); if the hole is irregular, shrink the rectangle and fill the gap between rectangle and bezel with opaque dark glass-edge colour so that holds.
- Save as `public/tv/console.png` (RGBA). Keep the original as `public/tv/console-source.png`. Append the prompt to `docs/asset-prompts.md`.
